# Swiss company and job discovery — eight methods

Adapted on 11 October 2026 from [the Netherlands methodology](https://github.com/duvida13/Companies-Agencies_-NL-/blob/050437a62736a17234a7a7e518996681b9aa702f/context/methodology.md). Read [the master table](../directory/all-companies.md) before each pass; strengthen uncertain records as well as discovering new names. Include contractors, staffing agencies, trainers, partners and international providers with evidenced Swiss activity. Preserve unresolved leads; Luis decides exclusions. Do not rank personal suitability.

## 1. Local-language keywords and variants

Switzerland has German, French, Italian and Romansh national languages, with multilingual cantons. Use written standard German for web discovery, then local terminology and place names; do not rely solely on English or translate Dutch queries literally. [FDFA language overview](https://www.aboutswitzerland.eda.admin.ch/en/language).

| Search language | Core discovery terms | Company / recruitment modifiers |
|---|---|---|
| German | Höhenarbeit, Höhenarbeiten, Höhenarbeiter, Industrieklettern, Industriekletterer, Seilarbeit, Seilarbeiten, Seilzugangstechnik, Seilzugangs- und Positionierungstechnik, SZP, Arbeiten am hängenden Seil | Firma, Unternehmen, Dienstleistungen, Montage, Reinigung, Stellen, Jobs, offene Stellen, Bewerbung, Temporär, Festanstellung |
| French | cordiste, technicien cordiste, travaux sur corde / cordes, travail sur cordes, travaux acrobatiques, travaux en accès difficile | entreprise, prestations, chantier, recrutement, emploi, candidature spontanée, intérim, temporaire |
| Italian | lavori in sospensione su corde, lavori in sospensione, lavori su corda / funi / fune, accesso e posizionamento mediante funi, lavori in quota | azienda, impresa, servizi, assunzioni, lavoro, candidatura, manutenzione, risanamento |

Add unaccented variants: Hoehenarbeit/Hohenarbeit, Geneve/Genève, Zürich/Zurich. Swiss spelling generally uses ss rather than ß. Search non-.ch domains too: TEC-ROPE's Swiss domain redirected to .be without proving a Belgian office. Italian trials using only “lavori su fune” returned many Italian firms; “lavori in sospensione” plus Ticino/Swiss places found local leads. Generic “in quota”, height work or fall protection alone does not establish rope execution.

## 2. Adjacent trades adapted to Switzerland

Retain cleaning, facade/glazing/roof work, coatings, welding, industrial maintenance, NDT/CND/ZfP, telecom, rigging, rescue and confined spaces. Expand into rockfall and avalanche structures, slope stabilization, drilling/anchors, hydropower/dams/penstocks, bridges, rail infrastructure, alpine construction and forestry. Search Felssicherung, Steinschlagschutz, Felsreinigung, Lawinenverbauung; protection contre les chutes de pierres, purge, confortement, dangers naturels; disgaggio, consolidamento, paramassi, ancoraggi.

These sectors are discovery routes, not automatic rope proof. Mountain guides, climbing gyms, recreational rope parks, cableway mechanics, wire-rope products, scaffolding and generic riggers remain ambiguous until evidence identifies the work method. Forestry/tree climbing should be labelled separately from industrial rope access. The first pass found explicit electrical rope work (Stromer am Seil) and natural-hazard contractors, demonstrating why a pure IRATA/company-name query is insufficient.

## 3. Canton and regional coverage

Rotate canton, municipality, industrial area and Alpine corridor. Search bilingual place-name variants, e.g. Biel/Bienne, Freiburg/Fribourg, Sitten/Sion, Wallis/Valais and Graubünden/Grisons/Grigioni/Grischun. A nationwide service claim or a city SEO page is not an office or completed project.

Use this full **26-canton research checklist**: AG Aargau; AI Appenzell Innerrhoden; AR Appenzell Ausserrhoden; BE Bern; BL Basel-Landschaft; BS Basel-Stadt; FR Fribourg; GE Genève; GL Glarus; GR Graubünden; JU Jura; LU Luzern; NE Neuchâtel; NW Nidwalden; OW Obwalden; SG St. Gallen; SH Schaffhausen; SO Solothurn; SZ Schwyz; TG Thurgau; TI Ticino; UR Uri; VD Vaud; VS Valais; ZG Zug; ZH Zürich.

Prioritize German-language discovery across the German-speaking cantons; French in GE/VD/NE/JU and French-speaking FR/VS/BE; Italian in TI and Italian-speaking GR; regional multilingual checks in GR. For BE/FR/VS/GR, search more than one local language. Do not treat these suggestions as a completed sweep.

Explore Zurich/Winterthur, Basel industrial sites, Bern/Biel, Geneva–Lausanne, Fribourg, Valais, central Switzerland, the Rhine valley, Ticino towns and Alpine infrastructure. Record HQ, branch, postal address, project location and service area separately. [Coverage achieved and gaps](handoff.md).

## 4. Deep primary pages and documents

Read services, projects, team, careers, contact, legal/imprint pages and current PDFs. Follow an employer's linked advert rather than trusting its page title. Preserve publication date, closing date, project/contract dates and review date separately. A crawl date is not publication.

Alkana's June 2026 advert remained online in October despite a passed application deadline. Toprope's linked PDF established mandatory SZP L3 and an 80% mainly-office project role; older merged wording said 80–100%. These are reasons to inspect detail documents. If extraction fails, try a different primary page or company-authored public post; label indexed-only evidence and failed access. Do not interpret failure as absence of capability.

## 5. Partners, brands, groups and identities

Follow named delivery partners, project contractors, trainers and suppliers. Record who supplies the rope crew, recruits and invoices where evidence permits. Logos/customers alone do not establish capability.

Resolve AG/SA, GmbH/Sàrl/Sagl, rebrands, trading names, branch locations and UID/CHE numbers using primary legal pages and the Swiss Zefix/UID registers. Registry identity does not itself prove rope execution. Distinct entities can share a domain; retain their relationship. Acro-Bât (.ch) and Acro Bat JDL (.com) must stay separate. Altix AG and Altix Academy AG share a site but are separately named. Vertical Access Group and VA Solutions are related, not interchangeable labels for every group company. FrisiTec/Stromer shared contacts do not justify an unverified legal merger.

## 6. Swiss associations, training and IRATA

Use [Suva's rope-work training overview](https://www.suva.ch/de-ch/praevention/lebenswichtige-regeln-und-bestimmungen/asa-richtlinie/ausbildung-fur-arbeiten-am-haengenden-seil), [Seilarbeit Schweiz companies](https://www.felssicherungen.ch/it/mitglieder/firmen), SHRV, ASRETC and IRATA directories. These are discovery routes beyond a Netherlands-style IRATA-only sweep.

Suva describes a three-stage Swiss SZP training framework and identifies recognized foreign training providers including IRATA, FISAT, SPRAT and France Travaux sur Cordes. Record the scheme and level exactly as stated in an advert; do not assume levels have identical duties across schemes or automatically substitute one certificate. PSAgA/antichute training is not proof of rope-access training. Keep training-only businesses visible with their type.

For company membership, inspect the actual current directory entry, category, reference number and review date. Employing certified technicians does not establish company membership. [IRATA verification guidance](https://irata.org/page/how-to-verify-an-irata-member/). An association listing of a named level-3 person is a lead, not a fresh certificate-validity check. The initial pass did not complete Swiss IRATA/SHRV membership audits.

## 7. Hiring sources, historical job leads and social evidence

Begin with employer/agency careers pages; use jobs.ch, jobup.ch, JobScout24, Job-Room, regional sites and specialist cordist boards to discover adverts. Aggregators and shortened URLs need destination checks. Do not equate an agency's office with its client's work location, and never guess an unnamed client.

Read the Swiss section of the cumulative combined findings, its review links and all saved merge runs; the latest run alone omits leads. Preserve original title, URL, reporting agent and source date in [inherited leads](../jobs/inherited-leads.md), separately from [fresh checks](../jobs/findings.md). The inherited country label remains a source claim until actual location is verified. Multilingual versions with the same employer job ID are likely duplicate candidates; different URLs must not be silently merged without verification. Keep incomplete URLs visible for repair rather than guessing.

Record actual required language and proficiency, Swiss residence/permit wording, driving licence, trade qualification (EFZ/CFC/AFC), certificate scheme/level, work percentage, temporary/permanent arrangement, start/deadline and location where stated. Leave unstated pay and accommodation unknown. Switzerland has its own employment-permission rules; preserve advert wording and consult current [SEM guidance](https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/en/home/overview-arbeit.html) before advising on eligibility. Do not infer Luis's nationality or right to work, and do not exclude companies based on it.

Company-authored public project posts can establish execution when extraction fails. A personal profile alone is a weak lead. Open applications and permanent recruitment hubs are application routes, not individual live vacancies.

## 8. Combine evidence and preserve uncertainty

For each organization separately establish identity, Swiss office/project/coverage, capability, own crew vs partner execution, staffing, training, certification and hiring. Preserve contradictions and historical evidence. Keep named weak leads in the directory, except unrelated/ambiguous job advertisers held on the compact reference page for Luis's review. No subjective suitability scores.

Use stable CH-ORG IDs, an alphabetical table, website immediately after company, then LinkedIn, classification, Swiss connection, HR/application routes, general contacts, address/map and concise evidence. Never guess contact addresses, recruiter roles, locations or client names. Switchboards, sales, owners and directors remain labelled.
