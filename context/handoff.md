## Observed adaptations that worked

- German searches found industrial specialists, cleaning/maintenance and electrical rope work. French company terms found Romandie contractors beyond the inherited jobs. Italian “lavori in sospensione” found Compul and Campobase; broad “lavori su fune” queries were contaminated by Italy results.
- Seilarbeit Schweiz's company directory yielded rock-protection, training and forestry leads. Its listing of an individual's level must not be promoted to a current company certificate. More listed members still need inspection.
- Campobase's course address is hosted at Camillo Vismara; both have distinct roles. Vismara training does not prove a rope-contracting crew.
- Acro-Bât Sàrl (.ch, Valangin) is separate from Acro Bat JDL Sàrl (.com, Puidoux/Vernier). Similar names are not aliases without evidence.
- FrisiTec and Stromer am Seil publish shared address/phone and a partnership. Keep names distinct pending legal-identity checks.
- Vertical Access's group page resolves a former name and directs work to VA Solutions; training/safety/group brands should not inflate an operating-company count.
- RopEvolution has a longer association-listed name with the same domain/phone. Alias relationship is recorded, legal registration unresolved.
- Toprope's fresh PDF identifies an 80% project-lead role with mandatory SZP L3. The historical 80–100% title must not override it.
- Alkana's June deadline demonstrates why online visibility and agent report dates do not prove open hiring.
- Jobcenter's review-page URL actually exposes a specific temporary role. Preserve its ID query parameter; do not discard all query strings as tracking.
- Contractor city pages and recruitment hubs were misfiled as individual job leads in inherited material. Retain their discovery usefulness with corrected classification.
