## What changed for Switzerland

The approach now searches German, French, Italian and English, accounts for Romansh-region place names and multilingual cantons, and includes Swiss training/association sources alongside IRATA. It expands discovery into natural-hazard protection, alpine and civil infrastructure, forestry and hydropower while retaining urban/industrial trades.

The directory is an exploratory research database, not an exhaustive market census. Evidence strength, own crew vs partner delivery, membership, hiring and unknowns are kept separate. There are no suitability rankings. Company/contact information is maintained in one master table; public business contacts only.
